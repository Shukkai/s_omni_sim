# OS-V array packing

*Filling the idle 31 of 32 rows in decode attention*

Generated by `analysis/array_packing/pack_run.py` — re-run it to refresh.
Model: LLaMA-3-8B on Omni-LUT, 4-bit KV, standard attention.
Packs P independent attention instances into one OS-V pass, each with its own LGU driving `array_m / P` rows.

## Key findings

- Decode `attn_v` is `(M=1, K=kv_len, N=128)`, so `n_tiles = 1` and `rounds = 1`: **one of 32 PE rows works, at any context length** — 3.12% occupancy. Packing recovers exactly **32x** on that stage.
- **But 32x on the stage is not 32x on the token.** `attn_v` was compute-bound, so packing drives it under its own memory time and TPOT saturates at **P=8**: 1.755x / 2.637x / 3.118x at batch 1 / 8 / 32. P=16 and P=32 buy nothing.
- **Which makes it affordable.** P=8 with GQA-aware sharing needs 4.5 MB and fits 16 MB; P=32 independent needs 66 MB and does not. The two results meet at P=8.
- The result **rests on an unmodelled cost**: P=8 needs ~1.0 TB/s of KV-SRAM reads and the simulator has no bandwidth term at all.

## B. Two different sources of headroom

`qk` is evaluated at `kv_len = context + 1`, the first decode step, because that is what the array actually sees.

| kv_len | n_tiles | max P | idle rows | P=32 gain | source |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---|
| 1,025 | 9 | 32 | 71.9% | 3.56x | idle rows |
| 2,049 | 17 | 32 | 46.9% | 1.88x | idle rows |
| 4,097 | 33 | 32 | 48.4% | 1.94x | tail quantization |
| 8,193 | 65 | 32 | 32.3% | 1.48x | tail quantization |
| 32,769 | 257 | 32 | 10.8% | 1.12x | tail quantization |
| 131,073 | 1,025 | 32 | 2.9% | 1.03x | tail quantization |

> `attn_v` is not in this table: `n_tiles = 1` for any `head_dim <= 128`, so it is permanently in the 'idle rows' regime and packs 32x at every context.
> For `qk` the regimes split at `kv_len = array_m x array_n x NUM_RAC = 4,096`. Below it rows genuinely sit idle. At or above it the body is full and the only waste is the **tail**: `rounds = ceil(n_tiles/32)` rounds up to whole 32-row passes, leaving up to 31 rows idle in the last one. Packing recovers exactly `32*ceil(n_tiles/32)/n_tiles` — large just past a tile boundary, decaying as `1/n_tiles`. It is neutral **only** when `n_tiles` is an exact multiple of 32, and decode `kv_len = context + token_idx` almost never is.

## D. The co-residency that pays for it

Context 32,768, batch 1, decode peak SRAM. P instances means P working sets resident.

| P | packing source | independent | GQA-shared | fits 16 MB? |
|---:|:---|---:|---:|:---:|
| 1 | single | 2.1 MB | 2.1 MB | yes |
| 2 | intra-group | 4.1 MB | 2.1 MB | yes |
| 4 | intra-group | 8.3 MB | 2.3 MB | yes |
| 8 | cross-group | 16.5 MB | 4.5 MB | yes |
| 16 | cross-group | 33.0 MB | 9.0 MB | yes |
| 32 | cross-group | 66.0 MB | 18.0 MB | no |

> A GQA group shares its K/V tile, so packing 4 query heads is nearly free. Past the group size the tiles are distinct and the footprint grows with P — which is what would turn the 'free' 32x into a capacity problem. **Read against section C: the ceiling arrives at P=8, which costs 4.5 MB. The full achievable speedup is affordable; the 32x is neither reachable nor needed.**

## E. What the model does not charge for

Computed arithmetic, not a disclaimer.

| configuration | weight-FIFO / KV-SRAM reads | bandwidth |
|:---|---:|---:|
| unpacked (1 row live) | 256 B/cycle | 128.0 GB/s |
| P=4 (4 rows live) | 1,024 B/cycle | 0.51 TB/s |
| P=8 (8 rows live) | 2,048 B/cycle | 1.02 TB/s |
| P=32 (32 rows live) | 8,192 B/cycle | 4.10 TB/s |

> **The simulator has no SRAM bandwidth term at all** — capacity is enforced, throughput is not. Packing converts an idle-array problem into an SRAM-bandwidth problem it cannot bill. This is the first thing to check before believing the result.

> **LGU ungating.** Section IV-D gates 31 of 32 LGUs specifically to save power; P=32 ungates all of them. Cycles fall 32x, LGU dynamic energy rises up to 32x, and the energy model sees neither.

> **Energy neutrality here is an artefact, not a finding.** `os_v_energy_model.py:23` charges `n_tiles/array_m` and `omni_energy_model.py` divides M==1 OS energy by `array_m` — energy is *already* amortised over all 32 rows while cycles charge a full round for one. The two halves of the model disagree today, and packing is what would make them agree.

> Also uncharged: P live LUTs (16 entries x 128 lanes) plus a P-way segmented broadcast tree; per-instance output routing (`OUTPUT_CYCLES` unchanged at 2); and the scheduling tail when `batch x heads < P`.
